Click a mark to remove it (click again to restore). Click a ▽ to count it. Click anywhere else to add a breath; it snaps to a high within ±0.25 s if there is one (hold Alt/Option to place it exactly). Drag across the plot to create a phase. The height scale stays fixed while you scroll and re-fits when you zoom or jump. “Signal” (or the R key) switches to the raw, unfiltered recording to inspect the original data; breaths are always detected on the filtered signal.

Detection

A high counts as a breath when its rise is at least “min breath size” of the typical breath height (10th–90th percentile range) in the surrounding window. Breaths within the unclear band of that threshold are marked for review. Your manual adds and removals are kept when you change settings.
